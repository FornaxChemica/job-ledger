# Cover letter rules

The script enforces the number rule and the slot boundary. Follow the rest when writing the BODY slot.

- Start from `assets/cover-letter-template.tex`. Change only ROLE and BODY.
- ROLE is the official job title, not a broadened version.
- Use only confirmed facts and bank projects. Do not invent a metric, an employer, or a tool.
- One or two projects, chosen because they match the posting. Use the posting's words for skills the student actually has. The resume, not the letter, is what carries every supported keyword. The resume still carries the full bank.
- Do not claim the student meets a requirement the screening step left unknown.
- A few short paragraphs. No headings.
