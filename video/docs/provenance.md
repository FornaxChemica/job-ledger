# Asset and audio provenance

## Product and fixture assets

- Job Ledger source: https://github.com/FornaxChemica/job-ledger, inspected commit `40d9b0fa8ede8a0d24e902ee016dfdf4718b1e07`.
- Existing production files were not changed. `scripts/prepare-fixtures.py` uses its renderers and controlled-slot editor to produce film-only fixtures.

- `public/documents/Resume.pdf`, `Cover letter.pdf`: actual template-slot outputs compiled locally with pdfLaTeX. `Transcript.pdf` is clearly labeled a fictional coursework summary, not an official record. No real grades, identifiers, contact information or credentials appear.

## Brand

The provided blue symbol was inspected visually in chat, but the original downloadable bytes were unavailable at production start. `public/brand/manifest.json` records the final asset status. No approximation of the symbol has been drawn. Until the original is supplied, the render uses a conventional gray contact initial and a text wordmark. That is a known brand-fidelity limitation, not an alternate official logo.

## Music and effects

- Original 112 BPM electronic instrumental and restrained synthesized UI effects generated by `scripts/synthesize-score.py` with seed 1842.
- Source: sine/additive synthesis and seeded noise only. No third-party music, samples, recordings, reference-video audio, or cloned voice.
- Separate original music and effect stems plus the normalized master are in `public/audio/`.
- Score builds into tailoring, ducks for the separate submission approval, and resolves on the end card. Final AAC loudness and true peak are measured in `deliverables/audio-verification.json`.
- No voiceover is supplied: no suitable licensed natural voice was available. The film is designed to work on mute.

Nothing was uploaded or published externally.
